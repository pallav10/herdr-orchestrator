## Gotchas
- Workspace close is not worktree or branch deletion and deletes no source or data. Close only from that run's active controller, targeting the recorded worker_id or runtime identity with the exact holder triple, after explicit VCS consumption: promoted integration.json for integrators; current same-attempt controller success and a matching immutable consumed snapshot/digest for other consumers.
